# Business Insights

## Executive Summary
The dataset shows that upbeat and high-energy tracks are receiving the strongest engagement. This suggests that users respond well to songs with positive mood, strong tempo, and high familiarity.

## Key Findings
- Pop tracks dominate listening volume
- Top artists are often associated with energetic, feel-good music
- Users are most active during evening periods
- Personalized playlists are a strong optimization opportunity

## Recommendations
1. Create mood-based playlists for energy, focus, and workout sessions.
2. Prioritize high-performing artists in promotional campaigns.
3. Recommend similar tracks based on user listening habits.
4. Use AI-generated summaries to share findings with non-technical stakeholders.

## Strategic Value
This project demonstrates how analytics can be turned into actionable product and marketing decisions for a music streaming platform.
